Architecture habits

How I approach systems work

01

Source-backed delivery

I turn design/product sources into implementation rules, route boundaries, source-compliance checks, and regression tests so UI does not drift silently.

02

Backend boundaries

I prefer explicit data ownership through Auth, RLS, RPCs, storage policies, and server-side integrations instead of pushing sensitive behavior into clients.

03

Alpha honesty

I keep mock behavior, unconfigured services, and deferred product decisions visible until they are replaced, accepted, or removed.
